## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Extraction / Computer Vision Segmentation Pipeline

**Description:**
CrackVision AI is a specialized civil infrastructure vision system that inspects concrete and asphalt structural elements (bridge bents, precast bridge girders, retaining walls, slab decks, highway pavements) to autonomously detect, segment, and quantify surface cracking. Civil engineers, Department of Transportation (DOT) bridge inspectors, and structural asset managers upload high-resolution field imagery to the system. "Good" is characterized by exact pixel-level polygonal boundary delineation of crack fissures (even sub-millimeter apertures), zero confusion with formwork ties or shadows, extraction of calibrated aperture measurements conforming to ACI 224R-01 permissible thresholds, and actionable structural maintenance triage adhering to ASTM C881 protocols.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. **False Negative on Structural Shear / Tension Cracks**: Missing high-consequence diagonal shear cracks (45° near support bearings) or flexural tension fissures that indicate impending structural distress.
2. **False Positive on Non-Defect Features**: Hallucinating crack fissures from surface texture, formwork joints, rebar shadows, tire marks, tining grooves, or staining—triggering unwarranted, costly field interventions.
3. **Discontinuous Mask Fragmentation**: Fragmenting a single continuous high-severity crack into fragmented disjoint sub-polygons, skewing crack length and aperture quantification.
4. **Metric Hallucination / Aperture Overestimation**: Reporting inaccurate crack width apertures (e.g., misclassifying 0.15 mm hairline crazing as 1.5 mm structural spalling distress), causing invalid ACI compliance violations.
5. **Silent Model Failure / OOM Hang**: Crashing or hanging during high-resolution field image inference without graceful fallback to cached/deterministic processing.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge and civil engineering standards.

**Industry Vertical:** Civil Infrastructure Asset Management & Structural Forensic Engineering
**User Population:** DOT Bridge Inspectors, Licensed Professional Engineers (PE/SE), Field Technicians, Highway Asset Managers
**Stakes Level:** Critical (Human safety, catastrophic structural failure risk, multimillion-dollar rehabilitation budget allocation)
**Output Consequence:** Field outputs directly drive official Structural Condition Assessment Reports (SCAR), DOT National Bridge Inventory (NBI) ratings, repair work orders (epoxy pressure injection vs. structural shoring), and legal liability documentation.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

1. **Dimension: Defect Morphology & Structural Orientation**
   - **Good (expert accepts):** System accurately distinguishes between structural cracking (diagonal shear at 45°, flexural cracking at maximum moment zones) and non-structural cracking (plastic shrinkage, thermal curling, alkali-silica reaction map cracking).
   - **Bad (expert flags):** Conflating surface craze cracking with deep structural shear fissures, or classifying expansion control joints as structural distress.
   - **Stakes:** Critical.
   - **Source:** ACI 224.1R-07 ("Causes, Evaluation, and Repair of Cracks in Concrete Structures") & FHWA Bridge Inspector's Reference Manual (BIRM).

2. **Dimension: ACI 224R-01 Permissible Aperture Thresholding**
   - **Good (expert accepts):** Crack widths are categorized against environmental exposure limits:
     - Water-retaining structures: ≤ 0.10 mm (0.004 in.)
     - Deicing chemicals / marine spray: ≤ 0.15 mm – 0.18 mm (0.006 – 0.007 in.)
     - High humidity / moist air: ≤ 0.30 mm (0.012 in.)
     - Dry air / protected: ≤ 0.41 mm (0.016 in.)
   - **Bad (expert flags):** Generic "severe/moderate" labels with aperture measurements deviating > 0.10 mm from physical optical comparator card verification.
   - **Stakes:** High.
   - **Source:** ACI 224R-01 Table 4.1 ("Tolerable Crack Widths, Reinforced Concrete").

3. **Dimension: Mask Boundary Fidelity (Pixel-to-Polygon)**
   - **Good (expert accepts):** Segmentation polygons trace the true centerline and lateral boundary of the crack fissure without smoothing over sharp tortuous turns or widening into clean uncracked matrix.
   - **Bad (expert flags):** Blobs that enclose large patches of undamaged concrete or dilate a 0.2 mm hairline crack into a 20 mm wide segment.
   - **Stakes:** High.
   - **Source:** ASTM C823 ("Standard Practice for Examination and Sampling of Hardened Concrete in Constructions").

4. **Dimension: Remediation Protocol Accuracy (ASTM C881)**
   - **Good (expert accepts):** System pairs detected severity with correct remediation standards:
     - Aperture < 0.3 mm: Surface seal with low-viscosity epoxy or methacrylate penetrant (ASTM C881 Type I, Grade 1).
     - Aperture 0.3 – 3.0 mm (structural): Pressure-injected epoxy resin (ASTM C881 Type IV, Grade 1).
     - Active / moving cracks: Polyurethane chemical grout or elastomeric joint sealant.
   - **Bad (expert flags):** Recommending rigid epoxy injection across active working joints or recommending "routine monitoring" for wide structural shear fissures.
   - **Stakes:** Critical.
   - **Source:** ASTM C881/C881M-20 & ICRI Guideline No. 310.1R.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

- **Formwork & Joint False Positives**: Concrete tie holes, snap-tie indentations, form board seams, and saw-cut contraction joints exhibit strong linear contrast that naive vision models misidentify as active cracks.
- **Lighting & Shadow Confounding**: Direct solar shadows from bridge railings, overhead girders, or adjacent foliage create high-gradient dark bands resembling wide fissures.
- **Surface Texture Noise**: Broom-finished concrete, exposed aggregate, asphalt raveling, tined highway pavements, and pop-outs trigger dense clusters of microscopic false positive masks.
- **Tortuous Hairline Discontinuity**: Hairlines (< 0.2 mm) exhibit low contrast against weathered concrete substrate; models frequently drop segments, reporting 5 small independent cracks instead of a continuous 2-meter distress line.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

- **ACI 224R-01**: Control of Cracking in Concrete Structures (definitive standard for allowable crack apertures).
- **FHWA 23 CFR Part 650 Subpart C**: National Bridge Inspection Standards (NBIS), requiring systematic defect recording and element-level condition states (CS1 to CS4).
- **AASHTO Manual for Bridge Evaluation (MBE)**: Governs bridge condition ratings and load capacity reduction evaluations.
- **ASTM C881 / ICRI 310**: Concrete surface repair materials and epoxy injection protocol specifications.

| **Civil Materials QA Specialist** | Edge case validation on challenging substrates (weathered asphalt, exposed aggregate, stained marine piers) |

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** Ultralytics YOLOv8 Segmentation (`ultralytics>=8.3.0`, `torch>=2.2.0`, `torchvision>=0.17.0`)

**Version:** Pin `ultralytics==8.3.40`, `torch==2.3.1`, `torchvision==0.18.1` (or latest stable compatible release)

**Rationale:**
1. **Polygonal Mask Generation (`masks.xy`)**: Ultralytics YOLOv8-Seg natively outputs segmented polygonal boundary coordinates alongside confidence scores and bounding boxes in a single high-speed forward pass.
2. **Sub-second Local Inference**: Runs in 45–120ms on Apple Silicon MPS or modern CPU, eliminating the need for expensive dedicated cloud GPU clusters during field operations.
3. **Decoupled Architecture Alignment**: Integrates directly into FastAPI backend `CrackAnalyzer` (`backend/app/services/crack_analyzer.py`), preserving existing REST contracts (`POST /analyze`) while upgrading mock logic to real PyTorch computer vision.
4. **Pretrained Checkpoint Flexibility**: Supports loading fine-tuned weights (`crack500_yolov8s_seg.pt`) or base weights (`yolov8n-seg.pt`) with zero pipeline re-architecture.
5. **Retina Masks**: Enables full-resolution mask extrapolation (`retina_masks=True`), preserving thin hairline crack boundaries without downsampling pixel artifacts.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **SAM 2 (Segment Anything 2)** | Massive memory footprint (>2.5 GB VRAM) and heavy latency (>800ms) without bounding-box prompt guidance; prone to segmenting entire concrete slabs rather than isolating narrow fissure boundaries. |
| **Mask R-CNN (Detectron2)** | Complex compilation dependencies on macOS, legacy codebase, heavier inference latency (3x slower than YOLOv8), difficult C++ extension builds on clean environments. |
| **DeepLabV3+ (Torchvision)** | High semantic segmentation accuracy but outputs dense raster masks rather than instance-level polygons with bounding boxes, requiring heavy OpenCV contour vectorization loops. |
| **ONNX Runtime (Standalone)** | Feasible secondary export target, but using native Ultralytics PyTorch first allows seamless weight hot-swapping, test-time augmentation (TTA), and fine-tuning scripts. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** No. Open-source MIT / AGPL licensed PyTorch stack running locally on host machine. Zero third-party API dependencies or cloud credit lock-in.

### Entry Point Pattern
```python
from pathlib import Path
from typing import Dict, Any, List, Optional
import numpy as np
from PIL import Image
from ultralytics import YOLO
import io

class YOLOv8CrackSegmentationService:
    def __init__(self, weights_path: Optional[str] = None):
        self.device = "mps" if torch.backends.mps.is_available() else "cpu"
        # Fallback sequence: custom crack weights -> base yolov8n-seg -> mock fallback
        resolved_weights = weights_path or "weights/crack_yolov8s_seg.pt"
        if not Path(resolved_weights).exists():
            resolved_weights = "yolov8n-seg.pt"  # Auto-downloads base weights
            
        self.model = YOLO(resolved_weights)
        self.model.to(self.device)

    def segment_image(self, file_bytes: bytes, conf_threshold: float = 0.25) -> Dict[str, Any]:
        image = Image.open(io.BytesIO(file_bytes)).convert("RGB")
        width, height = image.size

        # Run inference with retina_masks=True to preserve hairline boundaries
        results: List[Results] = self.model.predict(
            source=image,
            conf=conf_threshold,
            iou=0.45,
            retina_masks=True,
            device=self.device,
            verbose=False
        )
        
        result = results[0]
        polygons: List[List[List[float]]] = []
        normalized_polygons: List[List[List[float]]] = []
        boxes: List[List[float]] = []
        confidences: List[float] = []

        if result.masks is not None and len(result.masks) > 0:
            # result.masks.xy provides pixel coordinates for each contour polygon
            for poly in result.masks.xy:
                if len(poly) >= 3:  # Valid polygon requires >= 3 points
                    poly_list = poly.tolist()
                    polygons.append(poly_list)
                    normalized_polygons.append([[round(x / width, 4), round(y / height, 4)] for x, y in poly_list])
            
            for box in result.boxes:
                boxes.append([round(float(v), 2) for v in box.xyxy[0].tolist()])
                confidences.append(round(float(box.conf[0]) * 100, 1))

        return {
            "has_crack": len(polygons) > 0,
            "count": len(polygons),
            "polygons": polygons,
            "normalized_polygons": normalized_polygons,
            "boxes": boxes,
            "confidences": confidences,
            "image_dimensions": {"width": width, "height": height}
        }
```

### Key Abstractions

| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `YOLO(model_path)` | Core Ultralytics model wrapper | Initializes PyTorch network, handles weight loading & hardware device mapping |
| `Results.masks.xy` | List of pixel coordinate polygon arrays `(N, 2)` | Primary output format passed to frontend canvas to render true segmented fissure overlays |
| `Results.masks.xyn` | List of normalized coordinate polygon arrays `[0, 1]` | Resolution-independent geometry used for responsive SVG / Canvas viewports |
| `retina_masks=True` | High-res mask rendering parameter in `.predict()` | Critical for hairline crack detection; prevents downsampling to `160x160` prototype masks |
| `Results.boxes.conf` | Detection confidence tensor | Triage filtering to reject low-probability surface artifacts (< 0.25 conf) |

### Common Pitfalls
1. **Default Mask Downsampling**: By default, YOLOv8 generates masks on a `160x160` prototype grid, severely degrading thin crack contours. Always pass `retina_masks=True` in `.predict()`.
2. **CPU Event Loop Blocking**: Calling `model.predict()` directly inside an `async def` FastAPI route blocks the asyncio event loop. Always offload inference to a worker thread via `anyio.to_thread.run_sync()`.
3. **Empty Mask Array Crashes**: In images with zero cracks, `result.masks` is `None` (not an empty list). Guard against `NoneType` access before iterating.
4. **Memory Leaks in Server Run**: Processing thousands of images without clearing PyTorch cache or running within `torch.inference_mode()` can cause memory creep. Wrap predictions in `torch.inference_mode()`.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**
- Architecture: YOLOv8 Small Segmentation (`yolov8s-seg.pt` or fine-tuned `crack_yolov8s_seg.pt`)
- Input Size (`imgsz`): 640x640 (balanced speed/accuracy) with dynamic aspect ratio preservation
- Confidence Threshold (`conf`): 0.25 (tuned to minimize shadow false positives while capturing fine fissures)
- NMS IoU Threshold (`iou`): 0.45
- Max Detections (`max_det`): 100
- Inference Mode: `torch.inference_mode()`, Apple MPS / CPU execution

**Core Pattern:**
1. FastAPI endpoint (`POST /analyze`) receives image payload.
2. Image bytes are validated for size and MIME type.
3. Handed to `CrackAnalyzer.analyze(file_bytes)` via `anyio.to_thread.run_sync()`.
4. `YOLOv8Service` runs inference with `retina_masks=True`.
5. Polygon vertices are extracted from `results.masks.xy`.
6. `MorphometryService` measures perimeter, bounding aspect ratio, orientation angle (vertical shrinkage vs diagonal shear), and estimated aperture against ACI 224R thresholds.
7. Structured Pydantic `AnalysisResponse` returned to React frontend with polygonal coordinates.
8. Frontend `InspectionViewport` renders SVG `<polygon>` vectors overlaying the original image canvas.

**State Management:**
Stateless REST API. Model weights are loaded once into memory upon FastAPI startup (`lifespan` handler) and cached in singleton `CrackAnalyzer` instance.

**Context / Resource Management:**
Image files are processed directly from memory buffers (`io.BytesIO`) without temporary disk writes. Heavy memory is reclaimed with explicit `del results` and `torch.mps.empty_cache()` / garbage collection on batch operations.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

### Structured Outputs with Pydantic

The API response model ensures strict type enforcement and backward compatibility with the existing v1.0 frontend while exposing the new segmentation metadata:

```python
from pydantic import BaseModel, ConfigDict, Field
from datetime import datetime
from typing import List, Optional, Dict, Any

class PolygonContour(BaseModel):
    points: List[List[float]] = Field(..., description="Pixel coordinates [[x, y], ...]")
    normalized_points: List[List[float]] = Field(..., description="Normalized coordinates [[x/w, y/h], ...] 0-1")
    confidence: float = Field(..., ge=0.0, le=100.0)
    estimated_aperture_mm: float = Field(..., description="Calculated crack width at maximum aperture")

class AnalysisResponse(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(from_attributes=True)

    analysis_id: str
    status: str  # "crack_detected" | "no_significant_crack"
    confidence: float  # 0 - 100
    crack_type: str
    severity: str  # "low" | "medium" | "high"
    estimated_width: str  # e.g., "0.4 - 0.8 mm"
    affected_area: str  # e.g., "3.2%"
    recommendation: str
    inspection_note: str
    annotated_image_note: str
    created_at: datetime
    
    # Enhanced Phase 03 Computer Vision Fields:
    has_segmentation: bool = True
    polygons: List[List[List[float]]] = Field(default_factory=list, description="Raw pixel polygons")
    normalized_polygons: List[List[List[float]]] = Field(default_factory=list, description="Normalized 0-1 polygons for responsive canvas")
    bounding_boxes: List[List[float]] = Field(default_factory=list, description="[x1, y1, x2, y2] bounding coordinates")
    defect_count: int = 0
    inference_time_ms: float = 0.0
    aci_compliance: Optional[Dict[str, Any]] = None
```

### Async-First Design
- **Event Loop Protection**: Deep learning inference in PyTorch is CPU/GPU compute-intensive and synchronous. Calling `model.predict()` directly in an `async def` route halts all incoming HTTP traffic.
- **Worker Thread Offloading**:
  ```python
  import anyio

  @app.post("/analyze", response_model=AnalysisResponse)
  async def analyze_image(file: UploadFile = File(...)):
      contents = await file.read()
      # Offload synchronous PyTorch forward pass to AnyIO worker thread pool:
      result = await anyio.to_thread.run_sync(crack_analyzer.analyze, contents, file.filename)
      return result
  ```

### Deterministic Graceful Fallback
If the PyTorch runtime encounters an unresolvable CUDA/MPS driver exception or weights are missing from the container, the service must never throw unhandled 500 errors. It automatically switches to `FallbackCrackAnalyzer` (the v1.0 deterministic seed engine), logging an operational warning while maintaining service availability.

### Cost and Latency Budget
- Hardware Target: Host CPU / Apple Silicon MPS (Zero cloud API inference costs).
- Peak Latency Budget:
  - Image Decode & Resizing: ≤ 30 ms
  - YOLOv8-Seg Forward Pass: ≤ 120 ms
  - Polygon & Morphometry Extraction: ≤ 40 ms
  - Total Target API Response: ≤ 250 ms (well within the 500 ms SLA)
- Memory Footprint: ≤ 450 MB RAM for model weights and inference tensor buffers.

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| **Minimum Aspect Ratio Filter** | Detected mask bounding box has aspect ratio < 1.3:1 and area > 1000px² (indicative of a circular tie-hole or blob rather than a linear crack fissure) | Automatically discard candidate mask as non-crack artifact |
| **Low-Confidence Floor** | Individual polygon detection confidence < 20% | Filter out of final response to eliminate background speckle |
| **Payload Size & Resolution Cap** | Incoming upload > 10 MB or resolution > 4096x4096 | Pre-scale image with PIL bilinear downsampling before PyTorch tensor conversion |
| **Driver / OOM Crash Protection** | PyTorch runtime raises CUDA/MPS out-of-memory or initialization exception | Catch exception, log telemetry alert, seamlessly route to deterministic fallback service |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Aperture Discrepancy Rate** | 100% of user-reported caliper adjustments in frontend | Add image and adjusted coordinates to training retraining pool |
| **Zero-Detection Ratio** | Daily log analysis of images where confidence was 0% | Sample 10% for inspector audit to identify uncaptured crack morphology |
| **Inference Latency Drift** | P95 latency metrics from Arize Phoenix | Alert team if P95 exceeds 500 ms on standard field hardware |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** Arize Phoenix (self-hosted local dashboard on port 6006) via OpenTelemetry FastAPI middleware.

**Key Metrics to Track:**
1. `crackvision.inference.duration_ms`: Latency of YOLO forward pass and polygon extraction.
2. `crackvision.detection.count`: Number of distinct fissures detected per survey.
3. `crackvision.detection.mean_confidence`: Average confidence score per inspection.
4. `crackvision.pipeline.fallback_triggered`: Counter monitoring instances where the pipeline degraded to mock engine.
5. `crackvision.aperture.max_mm`: Maximum detected fissure aperture across survey jobs.

**Alert Thresholds:**
- Warning Alert: `duration_ms` P95 > 500 ms over a 5-minute inspection window.
- Critical Alert: `pipeline.fallback_triggered` > 0 during production operations.
- Data Drift Alert: `mean_confidence` drops below 60% over 20 consecutive inspections.

**Smart Sampling Strategy:**
Log all inspections that trigger low confidence (between 25% and 40%) or have extreme aperture estimates (> 4.0 mm) into a dedicated triage directory (`.planning/eval_samples/`) for monthly expert review and dataset enrichment.
